## Shared invariants
Host/solo mutation authority; stopped editing except deliberately boundary-queued change. Invalidate asynchronous work on roomauthoritychange, syncrolechange, historyboundary, playbackstatechange and relevant DOM/schema changes. Server authority/capability negotiation and monotonic revisions preserved; no legacy client may falsely appear compatible with advanced transport features. All device-local mix/theme/latency/calibration/library preferences remain outside shared arrangement and credentials. Private recordings/custom sample data are not exported/shared. One range apply yields one history step. Never suspend/mute the whole AudioContext for practice gaps; preserve private auditions and irreversible source lifetime cancellation. Native pan/pinch before hold; stationary subdivision versus moving precision must share ownership, no concurrent timers mutating music.
